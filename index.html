Proprietary methodology

The selection and validation of each investment is grounded in a Sustainable Balanced Scorecard (SBSC) — a multi-dimensional model developed and academically validated in a master's thesis, to support investment decisions in industrial-focused funds.

Mandate

Agnostic approach, anchored in three verticals.

Flexible sector mandate, with opportunity mapping across three verticals of robust thesis, favorable timing and internationalization potential.

01
Manufacturing & Industrial

Nearshoring is relocating supply chains to Europe. Portugal attracts investment from Germany, France and the US.

Target segments
  • Precision mechanics and metalworking
  • Automotive and aeronautics components
  • Industrial plastics and molds
  • Equipment and industrial automation
02
Agrofood & Packaging

Portugal is a leading exporter of olive oil, wine and canned goods. ESG pressure drives demand for packaging and traceability innovation.

Target segments
  • Canned and processed foods
  • Olive oil and wines for export
  • Sustainable and recyclable packaging
  • Functional and nutritional ingredients
03
Defense & Dual-Use

NATO commitment accelerates modernization of the defense industrial base and demand for civil and military dual-use capabilities.

Target segments
  • Electronic components and systems
  • Protection vehicles and structures
  • Secure communications and cybersecurity
  • Advanced materials and composites
