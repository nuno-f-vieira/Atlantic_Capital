Investment criteria

What we look for in a company

Sound Portuguese industrial companies, profitable and exporting, where a change of ownership is also the moment to professionalise and grow.

Verticals

Sector-flexible within industry

Opportunities outside the three verticals can only proceed with a specific rationale and the unanimous approval of the Investment Committee.

01

Manufacturing & Industrial

Portugal has a broad, export-led industrial base: metalworking, components, plastics, moulds and equipment, with strong clusters across the North and Centre. Nearshoring brings new demand from European and US customers, and many of these companies are ready for a step change in scale and automation.

  • Precision machining and metalworking
  • Automotive (Tier 2) and aerospace components
  • Industrial plastics
  • Moulds and tooling
  • Equipment and automation

Revenue €5–25M · EBITDA €1–5M

02

Agrofood & Packaging

A strong export position in olive oil, wine and canned food. Customer and regulatory pressure for sustainable packaging and traceability opens room for consolidation and innovation.

  • Canned and processed food
  • Export olive oil and wine
  • Sustainable packaging
  • Functional ingredients

Revenue €8–30M · EBITDA €1.5–5M

03

Defence & Dual-Use

The modernisation of Europe's defence industrial base creates demand for dual-use capabilities. Reinforced compliance due diligence: export control, end users and information security.

  • Electronic components and systems
  • Protection structures
  • Secure communications and cybersecurity
  • Advanced materials

Revenue €5–20M · EBITDA €1–4M

Exclusions

Where we do not invest

  • Controversial weapons and anything under EU or UN sanctions.
  • Tobacco, gambling, adult content and thermal coal.
  • Companies with serious unresolved environmental, labour or tax issues.
  • In defence and dual-use, only legitimate uses and end customers in the EU, NATO or allied countries.
