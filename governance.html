Governance & ESG

How we decide, manage conflicts and invest responsibly.

Governance

Clear decisions, independent oversight

Investors trust us with their capital and founders with their companies. Our governance is designed so both can see how decisions are taken.

Independent oversight

A Supervisory Board and an independent member on the Investment Committee look over our decisions.

A clear decision process

Each investment goes through defined stages, and every decision is recorded.

Conflicts handled openly

Related-party matters are disclosed and decided by those without a conflict.

Responsible investment

ESG as a source of value

Industrial companies that use energy well, keep their people safe and are well governed are more competitive and easier to finance. We build ESG into each company's plan from the first day.

Environment

Energy efficiency and a credible path to lower emissions.

People & safety

Safe workplaces, training and fair conditions.

Good governance

Strong boards, internal control, ethics and compliance.
