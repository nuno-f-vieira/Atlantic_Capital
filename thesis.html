Investment thesis

Why the Portuguese industrial middle-market, and why now.

Investment thesis

Competent, exporting, profitable — and under-capitalised

The Portuguese industrial middle-market has technically capable, export-oriented and profitable companies, with little access to growth capital and professional management. Many are reaching a succession moment.

Atlantic Capital acquires control of these companies at disciplined entry valuations, transforms them operationally with its own playbook, and prepares them for a new cycle of growth with larger strategic or financial partners.

The thesis rests on three factors: a structural capital gap, a succession wave and a valuation advantage over mature private equity markets. What sets it apart is the team's operating capability and its access to proprietary deal flow.

Why now

Five forces converging

01Succession

A generation of founders who built their companies after EU accession is nearing retirement without a prepared family or professional successor.

02Nearshoring

Supply chains moving from Asia back to Europe favour Iberian suppliers with proven quality and competitive costs.

03European defence

Higher NATO defence budgets and the modernisation of the industrial base create demand for dual-use capabilities.

04ESG pressure

Customers, banks and regulators require decarbonisation, traceability and reporting. Early adopters gain access to contracts and financing.

05Valuation gap

Entry valuations in Portugal remain well below those of mature private equity markets. Companies that gain scale and professional management are valued accordingly.

01

Manufacturing & Industrial

Portugal has a broad, export-led industrial base: metalworking, components, plastics, moulds and equipment, with strong clusters across the North and Centre. Nearshoring brings new demand from European and US customers, and many of these companies are ready for a step change in scale and automation.

  • Precision machining and metalworking
  • Automotive (Tier 2) and aerospace components
  • Industrial plastics
  • Moulds and tooling
  • Equipment and automation
02

Agrofood & Packaging

A strong export position in olive oil, wine and canned food. Customer and regulatory pressure for sustainable packaging and traceability opens room for consolidation and innovation.

  • Canned and processed food
  • Export olive oil and wine
  • Sustainable packaging
  • Functional ingredients
03

Defence & Dual-Use

The modernisation of Europe's defence industrial base creates demand for dual-use capabilities. Reinforced compliance due diligence: export control, end users and information security.

  • Electronic components and systems
  • Protection structures
  • Secure communications and cybersecurity
  • Advanced materials

Exit

An exit that reflects the value created

The exit is the consequence of the value created during the holding period: a company with more scale, better margins, institutional governance and a team ready for the next stage. Most often it is the entry of a strategic partner or a larger investor who will continue the growth; a listing is a residual option.
